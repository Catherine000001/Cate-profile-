§ 01 — About

Helping businesses thrive online.

I'm a front-end web developer based in Nairobi, Kenya, building clean, fast, and genuinely useful websites for businesses that want their online presence to work as hard as they do. My focus is simple: every site I ship should make it easier for a visitor to become a customer.

I care about the details other developers skip — the way a button responds to a tap, the second it takes a page to load, whether a form actually makes sense on a small screen. Problem-solving is the part of the job I enjoy most: understanding what a business actually needs before I write a single line of code.

I've built a specialty in booking and reservation flows and WhatsApp-based customer contact, working closely with hotel, restaurant, and salon owners to turn browsing visitors into confirmed bookings. That same attention to conversion, speed, and clarity carries into every project I take on.

I collaborate remotely as a matter of course — clear written updates, honest timelines, and version control on every project so you always know exactly where things stand. I'm also a continuous learner: the web moves fast, and I make a habit of keeping my skills current rather than static.

  • Custom business websites tailored to brand and goals
  • Booking & reservation systems built directly into the site
  • WhatsApp integration for instant, direct customer contact
  • Mobile-first, fast-loading, SEO-friendly builds
